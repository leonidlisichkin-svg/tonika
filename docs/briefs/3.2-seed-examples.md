# 3.2 Seed examples

**Roadmap:** Phase 3. **Needs Leo:** reading a sample as a native speaker. **After:** 3.1.

## Goal

The decision lists need something to learn from before the first outside contribution arrives:
at least 300 original sentences for the most frequent Russian homographs, both readings of each.

## Read first

`docs/DATA_SPEC.md`, `docs/specs/dataset-loop.md` (sections 1 and 2), `CLAUDE.md` principle 9.

## Build

- Choose about 30 homograph pairs by how often they occur in ordinary Russian prose (замок,
  мука, дорога, стоит, уже, большая, все/всё, …). Choose by frequency, **not** by which words
  fail on the golden set.
- For each pair write at least five sentences per reading: natural, varied in position and
  neighbours, 5 to 20 words, the kind of sentence a narrator would say. Mark the target with `+`.
- Convert with `tonika data import` into `data/examples/ru/ru-0001.jsonl`, `origin: editor`.
- `tonika data check data/examples` must pass, including the golden-overlap check.

## Review with Leo

Show him twenty sentences at a time, as plain marked text, and ask only: «Есть ли здесь
ударение, которое звучит неправильно?» Three batches chosen at random are enough. Fix what he
finds; if a batch has more than one error, review everything for that word.

## Pitfalls

- Do not copy, paraphrase or mirror golden sentences. Do not open `data/golden/` while writing.
- Every sentence must be written for this project (CC0). Nothing quoted from books, films or the web.
- Where both readings are acceptable Russian (тво́рог / творо́г), leave the word out.

## Done when

At least 300 records pass the check, Leo has read three batches, CI is green.
