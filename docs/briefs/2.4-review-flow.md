# 2.4 Review flow

**Roadmap:** Phase 2. **Needs Leo:** a look on his phone at the end.
**Spec:** [`docs/specs/web-editor.md`](../specs/web-editor.md), sections "Choosing a reading",
"The screen" (Ready), "Quality bar".

## Goal

The author's attention goes only to doubtful words: see them, walk through them with one thumb,
choose a reading, apply it everywhere, keep a personal dictionary.

## Read first

The spec. The screen from 2.3. `docs/DICTIONARY.md`.

## Build

- Doubtful words as buttons with the dotted accent underline; decided words solid and quiet.
- The sheet: sentence with the word highlighted, readings as large buttons (acute accent and
  tinted letter whatever the engine), «Так везде в этом тексте», «Отменить».
- «Проверить»: the walk through doubtful words in order with «3 из 12», back and forward,
  «Оставить как есть». The counter «Спорных слов: 12 · проверено 3».
- «Мой словарь»: a sheet with the dictionary text, saved in `localStorage`, sent with every
  request; a server `dictionary_error` shown under the field; changing it re-analyses.
- Accessibility as in the spec: dialog semantics, focus trap, Escape, labels, keyboard.
- Tests: choosing changes the final text; undo restores it; "everywhere" touches only tokens that
  offer the same reading; the walk visits every doubtful word once.

## Out of scope

Sending anything to the dataset. Meanings of readings. Deployment.

## Pitfalls

- Never mutate the document from the server; choices live beside it.
- A chosen reading has `source: "user"` and is marked in every mode, as in Python.
- A long text has thousands of tokens: only doubtful ones are buttons.

## Done when

On a phone, a real script's doubtful words are reviewed with one thumb and the copied text
contains every choice. CI green.
