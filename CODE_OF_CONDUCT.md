# Code of Conduct

Tonika brings together speakers of many languages. Treat everyone with respect.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people writing in a language that is not their first.
- Disagree about readings and norms with sources, not with people.
- Assume good faith. Accept that some pronunciations are regional or genuinely disputed.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Deliberately wrong or offensive data ("vandalism").
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for people who break
these rules. Report problems by opening an issue marked `conduct`, or contact the maintainer
privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
